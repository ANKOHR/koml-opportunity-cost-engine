# Lessons for KOML

The best unchosen feasible task is the opportunity cost, while the difference from the chosen value is a separate avoidable gap. Summing every alternative would overstate the cost of one mutually exclusive decision.

The planted fixture validates exact arithmetic, tie handling, selection validation, and identification of dominated choices. Mutating a valuation changes totals by the exact penny amount. It does not validate the assumption that Henry can assign useful comparable valuations or that this tool earns daily use. That assumption remains untested until a real trial.

Operating rule: require explicitly grouped feasible alternatives and independently hand-computed expectations before trusting a control-layer comparison. Keep input provenance separate from the evidence status of its values; even real entered records can contain hypothetical valuations.
